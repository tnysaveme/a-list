# Source-use review — October 25, 2026

Scope: the owner and a few friends use A-List to save films and subscribe to personal release-date calendars. The owner clarified that this is not an unrestricted public launch, and requested no public support or permission-request workflow. No messages have been sent to providers.

## Findings

| Source | What A-List uses | Evidence reviewed | Permission conclusion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DVDs Release Dates | Digital listings, native movie identity, poster links and source links | [Disclaimer](https://www.dvdsreleasedates.com/disclaimer/), [site](https://www.dvdsreleasedates.com/), [privacy policy](https://www.dvdsreleasedates.com/privacy-policy/) | The disclaimer covers reliability and changing dates; it does not establish an explicit republication license. No blanket data/image reuse grant was verified. |
| When To Stream | Recent RSS reports, native film profiles, sourced digital/subscription dates | [Site](https://whentostream.com/), [RSS](https://whentostream.com/feed/) | A public RSS endpoint and linked articles are accessible. Their availability does not establish a blanket republication license. No explicit permission for broad redistribution was verified. |
| Cinemeta and poster hosts | Film identifiers, credits, synopses and poster URLs | [Official Cinemeta usage documentation](https://github.com/Stremio/stremio-addons/blob/master/docs/tutorial/using-cinemeta.md), [official metadata guide](https://stremio.github.io/stremio-addon-guide/step4), [Cinemeta](https://cinemeta.strem.io/stremio/v1) | Documentation describes integration, including metadata use in add-ons. This does not establish ownership or a general reuse license for all upstream movie descriptions and artwork. |

A-List keeps attribution and links to the release and synopsis sources. It stores structured release evidence rather than republishing whole articles. Shared caches and bounded background work reduce requests; sign-in users share those results. Source dates remain third-party listings, with rumors marked tentative.

Small-group use is the intended operating scope, not proof of a legal exemption or permission. Explicit rights remain unconfirmed. If the project later becomes a broad public or commercial service, resolve source and artwork permissions or adopt a provider with suitable published licensing before promoting that use. No external approval is represented as obtained by this review.
